## 2 · Regulatory Portals & Public Reference Sources
SatarkPay integrates deep-linking, reporting templates, and deterministic format validation for public grievance redressal and regulatory frameworks:
- **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP / CFCFRMS 1930):** Public helpline `1930` and portal `cybercrime.gov.in`.
- **SEBI SCORES 2.0:** Securities and Exchange Board of India online dispute and complaint resolution (`scores.gov.in`).
- **SMART ODR Portal:** Securities Market Approach for Resolution Through ODR Portal (`smartodr.in`).
- **DoT Sanchar Saathi (Chakshu / TAFCOP):** Department of Telecommunications suspected fraud communication reporting (`sancharsaathi.gov.in`).
- **Public Advisory Feeds:** RBI notifications, SEBI investor warnings, and PIB Fact Check public press releases.

No proprietary data or scraping is performed; user actions route through official, publicly published portals and contact endpoints.

## 3 · Open-Source Dependencies & Libraries
SatarkPay utilizes the following open-source libraries under their respective permissive licenses:
- **Android Runtime:** Android Jetpack libraries, Jetpack Compose, Material 3, AndroidX Room DB (Apache 2.0).
