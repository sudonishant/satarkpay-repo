## 5 · Immediate steps (complainant ke liye checklist)

1. 1930 par call karo — turant (golden hour). Transaction ID/UTR ready rakho.
2. cybercrime.gov.in par 'Report Cyber Financial Fraud' file karo, acknowledgement number save karo.
3. NCRP 'Suspect Repository Search' me dono UPI IDs / numbers daal kar check karo.
4. PhonePe app me dono transactions par Help/Report Issue — 'money transferred to fraud' dispute raise karo.
