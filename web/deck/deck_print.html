SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
Privacy By Design: DPDP Act 2023 Conformance
ZERO COMPROMISE
❌ What SatarkPay Strictly Avoids
  • No Continuous Screen Recording: SatarkPay never captures screen video or reads private chats.
  • No Invasive Android Permissions: Avoids READ_SMS, READ_CONTACTS, or QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES.
  • No Accessibility Scraping: Strictly rejects malware-like accessibility service abuse.
  • No Raw PII Storage: Screenshots are purged post-OCR; only entity hashes are kept.
✅ Architectural Privacy Guarantees
  • Client-Side Redaction (R28): Mobile numbers, VPAs, and Aadhaar masked on-device.
  • User-Triggered Actions: No auto-sending of emails or calls; user triggers every transmission.
  • Cryptographic Integrity: SHA-256 tamper-evident evidence chain stored in private sandbox.
  • Public Good Ethos: Zero monetization funnels, zero trading tips, zero financial data selling.
