Check a link

This does not use a blacklist. A blacklist is out of date the moment a domain is registered, and attackers register thousands a day. Instead the link is taken apart and compared, structurally, against the real domain it is imitating.

Why structure beats a list

What a link can lie about

Everything visible. The text you read, the blue underline, the padlock, the subdomain, even the letters themselves — Unicode lets а (Cyrillic) sit where a (Latin) should be and look identical. Only the registered domain is ultimately yours to check, and that is what this page pulls out first.

The four structural tells

A brand glued to an action wordBanks do not name their own site hdfc-login. The hyphen is the tell.
Digits inside a brandsbi24, hdfc2026 — a number is added because the plain name was taken.
A cheap bulk TLD.xyz, .top, .club, .icu cost almost nothing and are bought in bulk for pages that live for a week.
PunycodeA domain starting xn-- uses look-alike foreign characters. It renders as the brand and is a different name entirely.
