Evidence pack

Filing is where most people stall. They know something happened, but they cannot say it in the order a portal asks for it, and they are afraid of pasting their account number into a form. This page produces the record — structured, in plain English, and masked.

Read this before you use it. This is not a police complaint and it is not legal evidence by itself. It is a structured note of what you saw, in your own words. When you file, attach your bank statement and the original message from your own phone — those are the documents that count.

What happened

The record appears here

Nothing is sent anywhere. The text is assembled in this page, masked, and shown to you before you copy it.

Masking applied to every field
account numbers card numbers UPI handles email addresses OTP and PIN digits

Masking keeps the first few characters so the record is still readable and still identifiable to you — enough for an officer to match against your statement, not enough to be reused by someone else.

Filing, step by step

Call 1930 first if money has moved.The helpline can request a transaction hold. This is time-sensitive — the same day matters more than a perfect write-up.
File at cybercrime.gov.in.Choose the financial-fraud category. Keep your bank statement and the original message ready as attachments.
Tell your bank in writing.Use the fraud number on your card or the bank website. Ask for the complaint reference number and note it down.
Report the sender.Forward the SMS to 1909 to get the number blocked for everyone, not just for you.
Keep everything.Do not delete the message or the chat, even after filing. Do not pay any "recovery agent" who contacts you afterwards — that is the same crime again, aimed at people who have already been hit.
