The one idea this is built on

Fraud is not an event. It is a sequence — a call, a new payee, a strange handle, an amount larger than usual, arriving at the worst possible moment. Any one of those alone is survivable. Two or three together is the shape of an active attack, and that shape is visible on the device.

How a check runs

A signal firesYou are on a call. The payee is new. The handle is not a real PSP code.
The weights are added upWhole numbers, so you can do the arithmetic yourself and reach the same tier the app shows.
A pause appears, or the action is withheld30 seconds at CAUTION, 60 at HIGH. At CRITICAL there is no pay button at all.
You are offered a different routeOne that still completes the real goal — verify, split the amount, share instead of scan.

Tiers, and what each one does

LOW · 0–2 Nothing structural. Pay when ready — the app still tells you to match the name your app shows against the name you were given.
CAUTION · 3–5 A 30-second cooling-off. Long enough to read the signals, short enough to not feel punished.
HIGH · 6–9 A 60-second cooling-off and a written alternative route for every signal that fired.
CRITICAL · 10+ The pay action is withheld, and no countdown is shown — a countdown would imply it becomes available.

Privacy, stated as a fact rather than a promise

What stays on the phone

Everything. Messages you paste, links you check, payee handles, amounts, the verdicts. There is no backend to send them to — this is a set of static files running a rules engine in your browser, and it keeps working with the network off.

What still gets masked

Even with nothing to upload, identifiers are masked before they reach the screen or your clipboard: account numbers, card numbers, UPI handles, emails, and any OTP or PIN inside pasted text. Links are left intact on purpose — they are the most useful thing you can hand an investigator. See it working.

What this build is. A working prototype, not a licensed product. It does not move money, does not connect to your bank, and cannot see your account. Where a capability needs data this build does not have, it says so instead of guessing — the full list is on the capability page.
